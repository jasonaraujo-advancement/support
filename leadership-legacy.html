Leadership & Legacy

Leadership gifts become something specific in the world: an instrument, a named fellowship, an endowed fund, or a program that runs for years. Planned gifts carry that support beyond a lifetime.

Your leadership

Give where it matters most to you

Whether you want your gift to work everywhere at once or to build one thing that lasts for generations, your leadership shapes what SAO discovers next.

Make an unrestricted gift

Your unrestricted gift is the most flexible support you can give. It goes to the SAO Director's discretionary fund, where it can be put to work right away, wherever the need is sharpest that year.

Federal funding and grants cover much of SAO's core science, but they are slow, restricted, and reserved for work that is already proven. Your gift fills the gaps: the pilot study that earns the grant, the trip that gets a student to a telescope, the repair that keeps an instrument on the sky.

You can give any amount, once or as a recurring gift.

Create something that lasts

At the leadership level, your gift becomes something specific in the world: an instrument, a fellowship, a program that runs for years because you decided it should exist.

  • Named funds and endowments

    Establish a fund that carries your name, or your family's, in perpetuity, paying out every year to the research area, facility, or public program you care about most.

  • Fellowships and early-career support

    Name a fellowship for graduate students or postdoctoral researchers and fund the first independent years of a scientific career.

  • Instrumentation and missions

    Help build the telescopes, detectors, and instruments behind SAO's next discoveries, including the Giant Magellan Telescope, the Black Hole Explorer mission, and the next-generation Event Horizon Telescope.

  • Planned and legacy giving

    Through a bequest, charitable trust, or beneficiary designation, your support for the Observatory can continue beyond your lifetime. Contact SAO_Advancement@si.edu

Tribute and memorial gifts

Honor someone you love — a birthday, a retirement, the teacher who started it all — or remember someone who is gone. A tribute gift is a lasting way to celebrate a shared love of astronomy.

Step 1

Choose where your gift goes

Direct your tribute gift to any area of SAO's work: unrestricted support, a specific observatory or mission, education programs, or an existing named fund.

Step 2

Tell us who you are honoring

Share the person's full name as you would like it to appear, and let us know whether your gift is in honor of or in memory of them.

Step 3

Let us share the news

Give us the name and address of the person you would like us to notify. We never share the amount of your gift unless you ask us to.

Step 4

Receive your acknowledgment

You will receive a receipt for your records, and the person you named will receive a separate letter letting them know about your gift.
