The partnership experience

Built on three pillars

Partnerships offer direct, tiered access to the Observatory's people, facilities, and programs.

  • Pillar 1

    Access

    Briefings with scientists and research leaders, program invitations, and facility visits.

  • Pillar 2

    Engagement

    Custom virtual or in-person discussions tied to your organization's interests, and opportunities to connect your technical teams with our researchers.

  • Pillar 3

    Visibility

    Recognition across Smithsonian platforms and acknowledgment in publications and events.

Partnership levels

Six levels, three bands

Engagements are based on availability and aligned with scientific priorities. Each level includes the benefits of the level below it.
