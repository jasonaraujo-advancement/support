Assets

Stock and appreciated securities

Giving appreciated securities held more than one year can let a donor deduct the full fair market value and avoid capital gains tax on the appreciation. Confirm the treatment of any gift with your own tax advisor.

  1. Ask your broker to include your nameBrokers will not release your name unless you instruct them to. Unidentified securities are held for 24 months, then go to the purpose you designated, or to general support.
  2. Notify AdvancementSend the security name, ticker, number of shares, and expected transfer date to SAO_Advancement@si.edu.
  3. Instruct your brokerProvide the DTC details below. Direct the transfer to the Smithsonian Institution — not to the Observatory or a named program — or processing will be delayed.
  4. Confirm valuation with your advisorThe Smithsonian does not take a position on the value of donated securities.
  5. Receive your acknowledgment

Grant

Donor-advised funds

Recommend a grant to SAO from your account at Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard Charitable, a community foundation, or another sponsor.

Search "Smithsonian Institution"

In your DAF portal, search for Smithsonian Institution. Do not search for "Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory" — that returns an error. Designate SAO in the memo field instead.

  1. Log in to your DAF sponsorStart a new grant recommendation.
  2. Search for the recipientSearch Smithsonian Institution, or EIN 53-0206027.
  3. Add the designation in the memo"Designated to the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory", plus any further detail.
  4. Do not request benefitsIRS rules limit using DAF distributions for memberships that provide benefits, and for event tickets. [TO CONFIRM — which SNAC levels carry disqualifying benefits]
  5. Release your nameShare your name and address with the recipient so the gift can be credited and acknowledged to you.

Your DAF may send funds by check (see Give by check) or wire (see Wire transfer or ACH).

Age 70½+

IRA qualified charitable distributions

Donors aged 70½ and older can direct a distribution from a traditional IRA to charity. It is excluded from taxable income and can count toward a required minimum distribution.

  1. Confirm eligibility with your advisorAge, account type, and annual limits apply.
  2. Instruct your IRA custodianThe distribution must go directly from the custodian to the charity — a distribution paid to you first does not qualify.
  3. Give your custodian the recipient detailsSmithsonian Institution, EIN 53-0206027. Checks go to the address under Give by check; wires use the details under Wire transfer or ACH. Ask that "Designated to the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory" appear on the remittance.
  4. Tell us it is comingCustodian checks often arrive with no donor name. Email SAO_Advancement@si.edu with your name, the custodian, and the amount.
  5. Note the benefit ruleIRS rules limit using QCDs for memberships that provide benefits, and for event tickets.

Planned and legacy gifts
