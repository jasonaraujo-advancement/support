What we do

From nearby planets to the structure of the cosmos

"The sky belongs to everyone." That idea has guided SAO since 1890. Its work spans the full scale of the universe — combining observation, theory, and instrumentation — and is driven by fundamental questions about how the universe forms, evolves, and operates.

Research areas

  • Our Solar System

  • Stars, planets, and their origins

  • Galaxies and large-scale structure

  • Cosmology and the early universe

  • Laboratory astrophysics

  • Extreme astrophysics

What corporate support builds

  • Instruments

    The design and construction of telescopes, detectors, and scientific instruments.

  • Facilities

    Observatories and research facilities in Cambridge, Arizona, Hawaiʻi, and at international sites.

  • People

    The research of graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career scientists.

  • Education

    National science education programs for schools and educators.

The partnership experience

Built on three pillars

Partnerships offer direct, tiered access to the Observatory's people, facilities, and programs.

  • Pillar 1

    Access

    Briefings with scientists and research leaders, program invitations, and facility visits.

  • Pillar 2

    Engagement

    Custom virtual or in-person discussions tied to your organization's interests, and opportunities to connect your technical teams with our researchers.

  • Pillar 3

    Visibility

    Recognition across Smithsonian platforms and acknowledgment in publications and events.

Partnership levels

Six levels, three bands

Engagements are based on availability and aligned with scientific priorities. Each level includes the benefits of the level below it.

Premium engagement

For partners seeking lasting impact

The Corporate Leadership Council

Available exclusively to Vanguard and Luminary partners. Senior representatives from partner organizations receive highly private briefings, access to exclusive Observatory networks, and select opportunities for deeper, strategic engagement with our leadership.

Naming opportunities

For permanent, long-term visibility, the Observatory offers naming opportunities that connect a partner's name to the places and programs where research is carried out:

  • Research and engineering spaces
  • Observatories, facilities, and physical sites
  • Data and scientific programs
  • Fellowships and endowed positions
